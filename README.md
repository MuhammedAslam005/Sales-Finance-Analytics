## Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive Sales and Finance Analytics solution built for AtliQ Hardwares — a global hardware company selling products across 23+ markets worldwide.

The project was completed as part of the Codebasics Data Analytics Bootcamp 5.0 under the mentorship of Dhaval Patel Sir and Hemanand Vadivel Sir.


 All analysis was performed on a dataset containing 1 million+ rows of sales and financial data using Microsoft Excel, Power Query, Power Pivot, and DAX Measures.

 ##  Tools & Techniques Used

**📊 Microsoft Excel** — Primary analysis and reporting tool

**⚙️ Power Query** — Data cleaning, transformation and merging multiple sources

**🔗 Power Pivot** — Data modeling and relationship management (Star Schema)

**📐 DAX Measures** — Custom KPI calculations
- Net Sales, Gross Margin %, YoY Growth %
- DIVIDE function, calculated columns

**📋 Pivot Tables** — Dynamic summarization, Top N filtering, Value filters

**🎨 Conditional Formatting** — Visual highlighting of performance metrics




## Business Problem

AtliQ Hardwares needed a data-driven solution to:


📈 Track Net Sales Performance across customers and markets
🎯 Evaluate Market Performance vs Target for strategic planning
💰 Analyze Profit & Loss (P&L) across fiscal years and markets
📦 Identify Top & Bottom performing products by quantity and revenue
🌍 Understand Division-level and Sub-zone level financial performance
🆕 Discover New Products launched in 2021 and their market impact

## Reports Built

**Sales Analytics**

1. Customer Net Sales Performance


Tracks Net Sales for each customer across FY2020 and FY2021
Calculates Year-over-Year (YoY) growth %
Identifies top performing customers driving revenue


2. Market Performance vs Target


Compares Actual Sales vs Target for all 23 markets
Highlights markets that missed or exceeded their targets
Uses conditional formatting for instant visual insight


3. Top 10 Products by Growth


Identifies Top 10 products by Net Sales growth from 2020 to 2021
AQ Mx NB achieved highest growth at 5,623.5% 🚀
Helps business prioritize high-growth product lines


4. Top 5 & Bottom 5 Products by Quantity


Compares products by total quantity sold
Top 5: AQ Master wired x1 Ms leads with 4.2M units
Bottom 5: AQ HOME Allin1 Gen 2 with lowest at 8.9K units



**Finance Analytics**

5. P&L Statement by Market (FY2021)


Comprehensive Profit & Loss for all 23 markets
Covers Net Sales, COGS, Gross Margin and GM%
New Zealand had highest GM% at 48.2%
India was largest market with $161.3M in Net Sales


6. GM% by Sub-Zone (Quarterly)


Tracks Gross Margin % across 6 sub-zones quarterly
Covers FY2019, FY2020, and FY2021
Identifies sub-zones with declining margins for strategic action


7. Division Level Report


Analyzes performance across 3 divisions: N&S, P&A, PC
PC division achieved highest growth at 313.7% in 2021
Helps management allocate resources efficiently

## Key Business Insights

🏆 Overall Net Sales grew from $196.7M (2020) to $598.9M (2021) = 204.5% growth

📱 PC Division showed highest divisional growth at 313.7%

🚀 AQ Mx NB was the fastest growing product at 5,623.5% growth

🌏 India was the largest market contributing $161.3M in FY2021

🇳🇿 New Zealand had the highest Gross Margin % at 48.2%

🆕 16 new products were launched in 2021 contributing $176.2M

📉 All markets missed their 2021 targets — Grand Total variance was -9.2%
